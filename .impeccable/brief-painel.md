## Scope

Dashboard page of "Mestrados Profissionais IFFar" (Claude Dashboard artifact). Visitor mode: Operate, with a persuasive job. Code-led (Operate surface; no image generation round). World pinned by the user: the demand-study campaign pieces in Figma.

## Audience and job

IFFar professors writing the proposals and IFFar management. Job: see in seconds that there is real demand, then see where it comes from, filtered by course of interest. End by reading what respondents wrote.

## Constraints

Charts only, no full response table. Course filter. Comments as speech bubbles at the end, attributed by vínculo and município only. No contact data. No portrait photos. Every number from the responses dataset or a dash.calc over it.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The dashboard is the campaign piece answered: the same green poster that asked "responda ao formulário" now shouts back the count of people who answered yes. Refuses the neutral grey card grid of a default analytics dashboard.

OWN-WORLD: Deep green field #0E3B22 owning the whole page; lime #C6E86B highlight bars holding uppercase Poppins Black; white Poppins Medium text; lime double chevrons; round "ENSINO PÚBLICO / GRATUITO / E DE QUALIDADE" seal; square-ended lime bars on a translucent white track; white speech bubbles with dark green text, every fourth bubble lime for rhythm, on a darker green band (#092C18) that sets the voices apart as the closing chapter. IFFar logo (white over the field, dark green over the lime footer) as the brand signature.

STORY: The reader learns how many people want each master's, that most would enter at the first selection, where they live and could attend, who they are; then reads their own words and leaves motivated to keep building the proposal.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left: the IFFar logo, then lime bar with the live interested count plus "PESSOAS", second lime bar "QUEREM CURSAR", white line naming the program (or "MESTRADO PROFISSIONAL NO IFFAR"), one sentence with first-selection count and last response time. Right: the GRATUITO seal. Below, the course filter as three chevron options in campaign typography, then a three-statement strip (first selection, municipalities, institutions).

FORM: Campaign poster grammar extended into a scrolling report; pinned by the user's brief, position 1 of 1. seed: none (direction pinned by the user brief; concept-seed roll not run)

Signature interaction: choosing a course re-tells the whole poster, headline number, bars sliding to new widths in one coordinated ease-out.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
